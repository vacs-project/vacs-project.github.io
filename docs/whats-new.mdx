# What's New

## v2.6.0

[v2.6.0](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/releases/tag/vacs-client-v2.6.0) lets you use joystick and gamepad buttons for every key binding, repairs calls on its own when your audio stops arriving, and brings full key binding support to Linux systems running X11.

A complete list of changes is available in the [v2.6.0 release notes](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/releases/tag/vacs-client-v2.6.0) on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs). Below is a summary of the most important changes.

### Joystick and gamepad buttons as key bindings

Every binding field in vacs now accepts a **joystick or gamepad button** as well as a keyboard key. That covers Push-to-Talk, Push-to-Mute and the Radio PTT key in the [Transmit Config](/settings/transmit), and accepting calls, ending calls and toggling RADIO PRIO in the [Hotkeys Config](/settings/hotkeys).

To bind one, click a binding field as usual and press a button on your device instead of a key. The field then shows the button and the device it belongs to, for example **Button 3 (VPC Throttle)**. Nothing needs to be installed or configured first, and devices can be plugged in while vacs is running.

This means a HOTAS throttle, a yoke, a button box or a handset can drive your calls directly, without mapping the device to a keyboard key through third party software first.

{/* TODO(screenshot): /img/settings/HotkeysConfigPage-joystick.png - Settings > Hotkeys Config with
    one action bound to a joystick button, so the field reads something like "Button 3 (VPC
    Throttle)" instead of a key name, and the "Joystick Devices" button visible in the dialog
    header. */}

Bindings are tied to the device itself rather than to the port it is plugged into, so they survive unplugging the device, a reboot, or moving it to a different USB port.

:::tip[Devices that block binding]
Throttles and button boxes often have latched switches that report as permanently pressed. Such a device instantly wins any binding you try to make, and you would never get to press the button you actually wanted. The new **Joystick Devices** dialog, reachable from the Hotkeys Config and Transmit Config dialogs, lets you exclude those devices from binding capture while keeping any bindings you already made on them. See [Joystick Devices](/settings/hotkeys#joystick-devices).
:::

---

### Calls repair themselves when audio stops arriving

Call audio in vacs travels directly between the two controllers rather than through our servers, which keeps latency low. Some VPNs, notably Cloudflare WARP and Tailscale, break that path in one direction only: the other controller hears you, you hear nothing.

vacs now detects this. When a connected call stops receiving audio for a few seconds, it re-establishes the call through one of our relay servers, which works even when the direct path does not. The call stays up while this happens; you notice a short gap and the status indicator briefly turning orange.

Both controllers need to be on v2.6.0 or later for this to work. If the other side is on an older version, or if the call is already relayed and audio still is not arriving, vacs marks the call as **degraded** instead: the status indicator stays orange and a muted speaker icon appears on the affected call in the call sequence, so you can tell the difference between a quiet colleague and a broken call.

{/* TODO(screenshot): /img/troubleshooting/degraded-call.png - The call sequence with one active
    call showing the muted speaker icon in the top-left corner of the call button, and the top
    status bar indicator orange rather than green. */}

There is also a new **Always relay calls** option in the [Call Settings](/settings/call#always-relay-calls), off by default. It skips the direct connection entirely and relays every call from the start. Only turn it on if you regularly have trouble hearing other controllers and cannot turn the VPN off or exclude vacs from it, since relaying adds a small amount of latency.

See [One-way audio](/troubleshooting/audio#one-way-audio-you-cannot-hear-the-other-controller) for the full picture, including what the relay servers are and what happens to audio passing through them.

---

### Full key binding support on Linux with X11

Linux systems running an **X11** session previously had no global key bindings at all, which meant Push-to-Talk and the call hotkeys did not work outside the vacs window. X11 sessions are now on par with Windows and macOS:

- Keyboard keys can be bound in vacs and work while another application is focused.
- The **Audio for VATSIM** radio integration is available, since vacs can now trigger the transmission key in the AFV client. Previously this integration could not be used anywhere on Linux.

Nothing needs to be installed or granted for this, it works out of the box on an X11 session.

Wayland sessions are unchanged: keyboard shortcuts are still managed by your desktop environment and Audio for VATSIM remains unavailable there, because Wayland does not permit either. Joystick buttons, however, work on Wayland too, and binding one replaces the desktop-managed shortcut for that action. See [Platform support](/settings/hotkeys#platform-support) for the complete breakdown.

## v2.5.1

[v2.5.1](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/releases/tag/vacs-client-v2.5.1) is a patch release that fixes vacs failing to start on some Windows systems and makes vacs recover on its own when your default audio device changes.

A complete list of changes is available in the [v2.5.1 release notes](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/releases/tag/vacs-client-v2.5.1) on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs). Below is a summary of the most important changes.

### Bug fixes

- **vacs not starting on Windows** - Fixed vacs failing to start when your audio device reports more than two channels, which affected setups using SteelSeries Sonar and similar virtual audio devices. This only affected v2.5.0.
- **Audio stopping after a device change** - Changing your default audio device in Windows no longer leaves vacs without audio. The affected audio stream is now rebuilt automatically, including when the default device changes several times in quick succession. Previously this could show an error and require a restart of vacs.
- **Speaker problems ending calls** - A problem with your separate [speaker device](/settings/audio) no longer ends an active call. Only your main output device failing does.

:::note
Changing your **output device** in Windows settings while a call is active still ends that call. This is why vacs does not let you change audio devices in the settings during a call. If your system default output device changes on its own mid-call, for example because you unplug a headset, the call ends and you will need to place it again.
:::

## v2.5.0

[v2.5.0](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/releases/tag/vacs-client-v2.5.0) decouples radio integration from your call microphone mode, letting you combine any [Call Mic Mode](/settings/transmit#call-mic-mode) with any [Radio Integration](/settings/transmit#radio-integration), and extends radio transmission playback to Audio for VATSIM.

A complete list of changes is available in the [v2.5.0 release notes](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/releases/tag/vacs-client-v2.5.0) on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs). Below is a summary of the most important changes.

### Independent radio integration

Previously, using a radio integration (TrackAudio or Audio for VATSIM) required setting your **Call Mode** to a dedicated "Radio Integration" option, meaning you had to give up Voice Activation or Push-to-Mute for your calls if you wanted a radio integration active.

The **Transmit Config** dialog has been reworked to lift this restriction. The top dropdown, now called **Call Mic Mode**, controls only how your call microphone behaves (Voice Activation, Push-to-Talk, or Push-to-Mute). The **Radio Integration** dropdown at the bottom is always available, independent of the Call Mic Mode chosen above - so you can, for example, use Voice Activation for calls while still triggering radio transmissions through TrackAudio.

<img src="/img/settings/Transmit-VoiceActivation-TrackAudio.png" alt="vacs Transmit Config with Voice Activation and TrackAudio combined" className="screenshot" style={{ width: "80%" }} />

The behavior of the radio PTT key and the **RADIO PRIO** button now depends on the combination of Call Mic Mode and Radio Integration you choose. See the [Transmit Modes](/settings/transmit#how-call-mic-mode-and-radio-integration-interact) documentation for a full breakdown of every combination.

Your existing configuration is automatically migrated to the new format on update. This should carry your previous setup over correctly, but given the scope of this change, we recommend **checking your Transmit Config** after updating to make sure it still reflects the setup you expect.

---

### AudioForVatsim radio playback

[Radio transmission playback](/interface/playback), introduced in v2.4.0 for TrackAudio, is now also available when using the **Audio for VATSIM** radio integration. Enable it the same way, in the [Advanced Settings](/settings/advanced#playback).

:::note
Only **RX** (received) transmissions are recorded, and support is limited: since AFV does not expose per-transmission metadata, recorded clips won't show a callsign or frequency. AudioForVatsim playback recording is also only available on **Windows**.
:::

---

### Bug fixes

- **Radio prio incorrectly set when ending a call while holding PTT** - Fixed an issue where ending a call while holding your PTT key, then starting a new call without releasing it, could incorrectly engage RADIO PRIO even though the key was originally pressed only for the call.
- **Desktop state overwritten on remote connect** - Fixed an issue where the remote store sync could incorrectly overwrite the desktop state when a client connected.

## v2.4.0

[v2.4.0](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/releases/tag/vacs-client-v2.4.0) introduces radio transmission playback for TrackAudio, a fast couple mode for faster frequency coupling, and several bug fixes.

A complete list of changes is available in the [v2.4.0 release notes](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/releases/tag/vacs-client-v2.4.0) on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs). Below is a summary of the most important changes.

### Radio transmission playback

vacs now lets you **replay recent TrackAudio radio transmissions** directly from the [Playback page](/interface/playback). Once enabled in the [Advanced Settings](/settings/advanced), vacs records incoming radio transmissions while TrackAudio is running and connected. You can then play them back at any time using the full set of playback controls — play/pause, skip, rewind, and continuous playback mode.

<img src="/img/playback/playback_controls.gif" alt="Playback Controls Demo" className="screenshot" style={{ width: "80%" }} />


Exported recordings are saved to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\app.vacs.vacs-client\playback\saved` on Windows and `$XDG_DATA_HOME/app.vacs.vacs-client/playback/saved` (or `$HOME/.local/share/app.vacs.vacs-client/playback/saved`) on Linux.

:::note
Playback functionality is only available on Windows and Linux systems using PipeWire. macOS is not supported.
:::

---

### Fast CPL

The CPL button now supports a **Fast CPL** mode, configurable in the [Advanced Settings](/settings/advanced). Clicking **FAST CPL** immediately couples all TX-enabled frequencies at once, without entering Couple Mode first. The button label changes from **CPL** to **FAST CPL** when this mode is active.

In addition, the default **Original** mode gains a new shortcut: **double-clicking CPL** while outside Couple Mode now also couples all TX-enabled frequencies at once.

---

### Bug fixes

- **Custom button border color contrast** - Custom button border colors now have improved contrast, making button states easier to distinguish visually.
- **Volume preview on wrong device** - Fixed an issue where the volume preview sound played through the incorrect audio output device.
- **Page navigation on menu open/close** - Fixed unintended page navigation triggered when opening or closing menus.
- **Decimal values in GEO_PAGE_CONTAINER_SIZE** - The `GEO_PAGE_CONTAINER_SIZE` configuration parameter now correctly accepts decimal values.
- **ATIS callsign support** - The radio page callsign validation now correctly recognizes callsigns with an ATIS suffix.
- **Incorrect app data path on Windows** - Fixed an issue where vacs resolved application data to the roaming profile directory instead of the local app data directory on Windows.

## v2.3.0

[v2.3.0](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/releases/tag/vacs-client-v2.3.0) introduces TrackAudio radio integration, call status tracking in the history list, and several stability fixes.

A complete list of changes is available in the [v2.3.0 release notes](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/releases/tag/vacs-client-v2.3.0) on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs). Below is a summary of the most important changes.

### TrackAudio radio integration

vacs now includes a dedicated **[Radio page](/interface/radio)** that connects to [TrackAudio](https://github.com/pierr3/TrackAudio) and lets you manage your radio stack without leaving the vacs window. From the Radio page you can see all currently tuned frequencies, toggle receive and transmit on individual frequencies, and manage your station list - all in one place.

<img src="/img/radio/radio_overview.png" alt="vacs Radio Page" className="screenshot" style={{ width: "80%" }} />

The Radio page is only available when TrackAudio is running and connected. If you are using Audio for VATSIM (AFV) or another transmit mode, the page remains inactive.

---

### Call status in the call history

The call history list now shows the **outcome of each call** at a glance. Each entry is marked as accepted, rejected, or not answered.

---

### Login confirmation page

After completing VATSIM Connect login in the browser, you are now shown a clear **confirmation page** confirming that login was successful, instead of a blank screen.

---

### Bug fixes

- **DA keys not callable until reconnect** - Fixed a server-side issue where station coverage was not correctly updated when a controller changed position, causing neighboring controllers to see your DA keys as inactive until a reconnect. This was a persistent issue that continued to affect some setups after v2.1.0.
- **Empty stations list when connecting early** - Connecting before the VATSIM datafeed had registered your own frequency no longer results in an empty initial stations list, further improving the online state of other controllers around you.
- **"Use default call sources" not applied on first startup** - The automatic default call sources setting now correctly takes effect when connecting for the very first time after a fresh install.
- **DA subpage buttons missing color** - Buttons on DA key subpages now correctly display their assigned background color.
- **UI freezes on Linux (Wayland)** - Fixed random interface freezes when running vacs under a Wayland compositor.
- **Missing call sounds after reconnect** - Call start and end sounds now play correctly when a reconnect happens during an active call.
- **Audio device fallback** - Fixed an issue where the playback device fallback logic could select the wrong output when the primary device became unavailable.
- **Call display not updating on remote control** - Fixed the active call display on the remote control not clearing correctly when a call ended with an error.

## v2.2.0

[v2.2.0](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/releases/tag/vacs-client-v2.2.0) brings custom button colors, a dedicated speaker output for ringtones and UI sounds, improved list scrolling, and more reliable audio device handling.

A complete list of changes is available in the [v2.2.0 release notes](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/releases/tag/vacs-client-v2.2.0) on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs). Below is a summary of the most important changes.

### Custom button colors

Station buttons on both the [GEO](/interface/phone/geo) and [Tabbed](/interface/phone/tabbed) layouts can now display **custom background colors**. Dataset maintainers for your FIR can assign colors from a curated palette to individual buttons, making it easier to visually group or distinguish stations at a glance.

<img src="/img/button_colors.png" alt="vacs Call Source Selection" className="screenshot" style={{ width: "90%" }} />

---

### Separate speaker device for ringtones and UI sounds

You can now select a **separate audio output device** for ringtones/chimes and UI click sounds, allowing you to separate coordination audio from notifications.

The new speaker device can be configured in the [Audio Settings](/settings/audio). If no separate speaker device is selected, all sounds will continue to play through your main headset output as before.

---

### Improved list scrolling

Scrollable lists throughout vacs, such as the [Telephone Directory](/interface/telephone#telephone-directory) and the call history, now feature significantly improved scrolling. Holding a scroll button triggers **continuous scrolling** instead of requiring repeated clicks, and a visible **scrollbar** shows your current position within the list. You can also **drag the scrollbar** to quickly navigate through long lists.

---

### Improved audio device handling

Audio device selection is now more reliable across application restarts. vacs now identifies devices by a **unique device ID** rather than just the device name, which prevents issues when multiple devices share similar names or when devices are reconnected in a different order. On Linux, ALSA device detection has also been improved to avoid issues with exclusive audio devices.

Your existing device selections will be migrated automatically.

## v2.1.0

[v2.1.0](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/releases/tag/vacs-client-v2.1.0) adds remote control support, new integration options for third-party developers, and several quality-of-life improvements.

A complete list of changes is available in the [v2.1.0 release notes](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/releases/tag/vacs-client-v2.1.0) on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs). Below is a summary of the most important changes.

### Remote control

The headline feature of v2.1.0: you can now **control vacs from a browser on any device**. Open the remote control URL on a tablet, a second monitor, or even your phone, and you get the full vacs interface - make and receive calls, change your settings and interact with the application - all without touching the main window.

This is especially useful if you don't want to use your primary screen for coordination and want a dedicated panel on a separate screen or tablet. See the [Remote Control](/using-vacs/remote-control) guide to get started.

Additionally, v2.1.0 exposes a **WebSocket-based remote control API** that third-party tools and developers can use to interact with vacs programmatically. A detailed spec of the [Remote Control API](/advanced/remote-api) is available for developers.

---

### Automatic default call source

When connecting to a position, vacs can now **automatically set your default call source** based on the position you are logged in with. This is configured in the dataset by your vACC and means you no longer need to manually select a call source at the beginning of each session in most cases.

If the automatic selection doesn't match your preference, you can still override it at any time by manually [selecting a different call source](/using-vacs/making-a-call#selecting-a-call-source). The feature can also be disabled entirely in the [Call Settings](/settings/call).

---

### Station assignment on GEO page buttons

Users of our GEO layout will be happy to know that buttons on the main page can now be directly assigned to stations, allowing for quick calls right from the geographic, customized layout. This gives FIR dataset maintainers additional flexibility in creating intuitive, custom call interfaces for their controllers.

---

### Token-based authentication for third-party clients

Developers can now authenticate against the vacs signaling server using **VATSIM OAuth Access Tokens**, enabling fully custom third-party clients that integrate with the vacs backend without needing to run the vacs desktop application locally. This opens the door for community-built tools and alternative interfaces.

---

### Other improvements

- **Clock now shows seconds** - The clock in the top status bar now displays seconds for more precise time tracking. Clicking the clock toggles between realtime updates (every second), a "zen" mode that only updates every 10 seconds, and the previous current day display.
- **Sorted telephone directory** - The [Telephone Directory](/interface/telephone#telephone-directory) is now sorted by position ID, making it easier to find specific stations.
- **Persisted zoom level** - Your window zoom level is now saved and restored across restarts. Previously, adjusting the zoom with `Ctrl +` / `Ctrl -` would reset when you closed and reopened vacs.

---

### Bug fixes

- **Automatic session recovery on connection loss** - If your internet connection drops briefly, vacs now automatically terminates the old session and reconnects cleanly. Previously, a flakey connection could result in an "already logged in" error that required manual intervention.
- **Fixed station handoffs** - When a controller changes position or goes offline, all other controllers should now see consistent updates to station coverage. No more relogs required for others to correctly appear as online or offline on vacs.
- **Consistent phone button state during calls** - Fixed an issue where a phone button could blink in an inconsistent state when an outgoing and incoming call overlapped. Outgoing call state now correctly takes priority.
- **Duplicate clients after VATSIM sync** - Fixed a bug where clients could appear multiple times on the [client page](/interface/phone/clients) if they changed position of primary frequency.

## v2.0.0

[v2.0.0](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/releases/tag/vacs-client-v2.0.0) is a major release featuring a completely reworked calling system and a brand-new interface.

A complete list of changes is available in the [v2.0.0 release notes](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/releases/tag/vacs-client-v2.0.0) on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs). Below is a summary of the most important changes.

### Station-based calling

The biggest change in v2.0.0 is how you initiate calls.

Previously, you would select a specific **client** - a VATSIM controller connected to vacs - to call. This meant you had to know which controller was responsible for a certain sector and find them in the list of connected clients. While already an improvement over the TeamSpeak and Discord workarounds, this was still cumbersome and different from how real-life coordination works.

In v2.0.0, you now call a **station** instead. Simply select the ATC station you want to reach (e.g., "TWR LOWL", "N1 ACC", "DEL LOWW"), and vacs will automatically route your call to the controller currently in charge of that station. Top-down coverage and sector inheritance typical to VATSIM are handled automatically, ensuring your call always reaches the right person. If a station is covered by multiple controllers (e.g., during a coordinator/mentoring session), they will all receive the call and can choose to answer it. Note that this is not a conference call - only one controller can answer and receive it.

This brings coordination much closer to its real-life equivalent - you dial a position rather than a specific person.

Additionally, you can now select the station you are calling **from**. This gives the recipient more context, indicating which region they should expect to coordinate with you on.

- **Single click** on a station you control sets it as a **temporary call source**. Your next call will originate from this station, then reset automatically.
- **Double click** (clicking the same key again) sets it as the **default source**, which persists across calls.
- **Clicking again** unsets it.

You can have both a temporary and a default source selected at the same time. The temporary source takes priority for the next call and then reverts to the default.

### Call sounds

v2.0.0 adds subtle audio cues when a call connects or ends. No more watching the connection status indicator to know when your call has been answered - you'll hear it right away.

### Priority calls

You can now mark calls as **priority** when coordination is urgent. Priority calls appear visually distinct on the receiving end and trigger a more attention-grabbing ringtone to ensure they don't go unnoticed.

You can now adjust the zoom level of the vacs window using the familiar `Ctrl +` and `Ctrl -` keyboard shortcuts. This is especially useful if you've resized the window and want to ensure the best readability and layout. `Ctrl 0` resets the zoom level to its default.

## Migrating from v1.x to v2.0.0

v2.0.0 introduces breaking changes that affect how clients communicate with the server. **You must update before you can connect** - older clients are not compatible with the v2.0.0 server.

Your client will automatically prompt you to update and will refuse to connect until you do. See the [Updating](/getting-started/updating) guide for instructions.

Your local settings and [Configuration](/advanced/configuration) will be preserved during the update and will largely continue to work as expected. However, the previous `stations.toml` and its associated custom stations configuration have been removed in favour of the new [interface](/interface/overview). Refer to our [Clients page](/interface/phone/clients) for details on the latest UI and how to configure it.

You can find a migration guide for the new client page configuration in our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/vacs-project/vacs/blob/main/vacs-client/docs/config/client_page.md#migrating-from-vacs-1x-stations-config).
